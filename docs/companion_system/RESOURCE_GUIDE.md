# What Makes a Resource Better — JFF Quick Reference

Generated 2026-07-27 from the server's real data (1,767 schematics + 1,927 item templates — every experimental weight line in the game). Full per-item detail: `JFF_Resource_Guide.xlsx`.

## The three rules

1. Every crafted stat is fed by specific **resource attributes** in fixed percentages baked into that item's schematic. A resource is "better" for an item exactly when it scores high on the attributes *that item* weighs.
2. Resource quality sets the **ceiling** — skill, buffs and rolls only decide where under that ceiling the item lands.
3. "Best resource" is always **per item**, never universal. A 950-OQ metal is wrong for a crafting station (which wants Conductivity).

## What to hunt, by what you're making

| Making... | Hunt resources high in... |
|---|---|
| **Weapons** | Overall Quality 53%, Conductivity 22%, Shock Resistance 21% |
| **Armor & clothing** | Overall Quality 50%, Malleability 22%, Shock Resistance 21% |
| **Components** (segments, barrels, droid parts) | OQ 37%, Unit Toughness 20%, Conductivity 16%, SR 11% |
| **Medicine & buff packs** | Overall Quality 65%, Potential Energy 17% |
| **Food & drink** | Potential Energy 30%, Decay Resistance 29%, OQ 22%, Flavor 16% — the one place OQ doesn't rule |
| **Ship parts** | OQ 41%, Unit Toughness 22%, PE 15%, CD 15% |
| **Structures & vehicles (deeds)** | Decay Resistance 31%, Unit Toughness 26%, OQ 21% |
| **Furniture** | OQ 51%, Decay Resistance 47% |
| **Crafting tools & stations** | Conductivity 86% |
| **Survey tools** | Conductivity 100% |
| **Instruments** | Unit Toughness 50%, Conductivity 50% |
| **Fireworks / camp kits** | Overall Quality 100% |

## Attribute codes

OQ Overall Quality · PE Potential Energy · CD Conductivity · SR Shock Resistance · UT Unit Toughness · MA Malleability · DR Decay Resistance · HR Heat Resistance · FL Flavor · CR Cold Resistance

## How this connects to the companions

The companion crafting pipeline already scores candidate resources against these exact weight lines when it picks stacks and claims resource deeds ("best resource for THIS item"). The upcoming real-roll + experimentation work spends points on these same lines, and the deferred "Best Resource Report" radial will show the server's current best live resource per line — this guide is the offline version of that answer.
